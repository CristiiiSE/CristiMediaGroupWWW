Are the apps collecting personal information?
Our apps leverage modern security technologies including encryption to protect your data. We are transparent about what is collected. Full details are available in our Privacy Policy.
Are your services safe for storing personal information?
Yes. We employ state-of-the-art encryption and tokenization techniques to ensure the safety of your information across all our apps and services.
How do I get started with your apps?
Simply visit our Play Store page, download the app that suits your needs, create an account, and you're ready to go. Setup takes less than a minute.
Are there any fees for using your apps?
Core features are free. Some advanced functionality may require a one-time purchase or subscription. Pricing details are clearly listed on each app's Play Store page.
Can I review the code before using an app?
Absolutely. Contact us via email and we'll arrange a code review. We believe in transparency and welcome security-minded developers to take a look.

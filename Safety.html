Safety

Studown user safety

Studown is built to support students in a calm, secure, and distraction-free environment. Your safety, privacy, and wellbeing are core principles of the app.

No social interaction

Studown has no chat, comments, profiles, or social features. Users cannot contact or interact with others, removing risks such as harassment, bullying, or unwanted contact.

Safe and appropriate content

All content shown in Studown is predefined and study-related. The app does not display violent, sexual, hateful, or harmful material of any kind.

Minimal data usage

Exam dates are stored locally on your device only. Studown does not require personal information such as name, email address, age, or location.

Anonymous analytics

Anonymous usage analytics are used to improve the app experience, such as when an exam is set or cleared. No data is used to personally identify users.

Optional notifications

Notifications are limited to motivational study reminders. You can disable them at any time through your device settings.

General audience

Studown is suitable for students of all ages. The app does not target, exploit, or expose minors to inappropriate content or functionality.

Need help or have concerns?

If you experience any issues or have questions regarding safety or privacy, you can contact support directly.
